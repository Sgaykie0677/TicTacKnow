Privacy Policy — Tic Tac Know Last updated: August 6, 2026 Tic Tac Know ("the App") is a trivia-based tic-tac-toe game. This policy explains what information, if any, the App collects. Information We Collect Tic Tac Know does not require an account, login, or any personal information to play. The App does not collect, store, or share names, email addresses, location data, contact lists, photos, or any other personally identifiable information. Game Data Game progress (such as the current score) is stored only temporarily, in your device's memory, for the duration of your play session. This data is not saved permanently, transmitted to us, or shared with any third party. Third-Party Services The App loads standard web fonts from Google Fonts to display text. This is a common practice for websites and apps, and involves only the technical request needed to load font files — no personal data is sent as part of this. If a future version of the App includes an optional live trivia-question feature, that feature may send a game category and difficulty level to a backend service in order to generate a new question. No personal or identifying information is included in that request. Advertising The App does not currently display advertisements. Children's Privacy The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. A version of this App designed for younger players ("Tic Tac Know Kids") follows the same practices described here: no accounts, no personal data collection, and no advertising. Changes to This Policy If this policy changes — for example, if advertising or analytics are added in the future — this page will be updated, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
